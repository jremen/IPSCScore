# Overview

IPSCScore is a competition scoring app for practical shooting, IDPA, multi-gun, action steel, and other shooting sports.

This help guide explains how to use the app. Use the sidebar to jump to any section, click on screenshots to zoom, and click inline links to open screens in the app or jump to related Help sections.

## Quick tour

- **Matches** — Create and manage matches. Each match defines a competition with its own stages, shooters, and results.
- **Cups** — Combine several matches into season-long standings using best-match Hit Factor or Points scoring.
- **Stages** — Stages (also called "situations" in some disciplines) are the individual scored exercises.
- **Scoring** — Enter scores per shooter per stage, on a desktop or mobile device.
- **Registration** — Manage the global shooter database, register shooters in a match, and group/pair shooters who want to share a squad.
- **Squadding** — Assign shooters to numbered squads and create shooter groups.
- **Disqualification** — DQ a shooter for the match (their scores become zero).
- **Results** — View results by division, overall, by stage, by category, and by tag.
- **Kiosk mode** — Show live public results full-screen on a TV or projector.
- **QR codes & devices** — Share results, squads, and scorer access, and approve or revoke scoring devices.
- **Output & backups** — Export results to CSV, PDF, HTML, or JSON; create manual database backups and use automatic desktop backups.
